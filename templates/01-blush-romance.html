Questions

What should I wear?

Traditional Myanmar attire or formal wear. Soft pastels — blush, champagne, sage — are very welcome.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, free parking is available on site and attendants will guide you. Spaces are limited, so taxis or ride-hailing are a great option.

Can I bring my children?

Of course — little ones are welcome. Please include them in your guest count when you RSVP.
