01 — Feature

How they met

In conversation with Aung & Thiri, on seven years, one very long bus ride and a question asked at sunrise.

2019 — The beginning

Where did it all begin?

AungOn a delayed overnight bus from Yangon to Bagan. Thiri had the window seat and a bag of tamarind sweets; I had nothing to read. By morning we had swapped numbers and our favourite books.

2022 — The first trip

Your first trip together?

ThiriInle Lake in the rainy season. We were soaked, lost our umbrella to the wind and laughed the whole boat ride back. That was when I knew every trip with him would be an adventure.

2025 — The question

And the proposal?

AungAt sunrise, back where it started, with the balloons drifting over Bagan. I had rehearsed a speech for weeks and forgot every word of it.

07 — Questions

Good to know

Anything else? Message us any time.

What should I wear?

For the morning ceremony, traditional Myanmar attire (longyi or htamein) or formal wear in soft neutrals. For the evening reception, black tie optional. We kindly ask guests to avoid all-white outfits.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes — free on-site parking with attendants on duty all day. For the evening, we recommend a taxi or ride-hailing; drivers can drop off at the main gate.

Can I bring my children?

Of course. Little ones are warmly welcome at both the ceremony and the reception — please include them in your guest count when you RSVP.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you would like to give something, a card or a contribution towards our honeymoon would be lovely — there will be a gift table at the reception.
