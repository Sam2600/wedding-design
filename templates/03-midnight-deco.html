Chapter VII

Questions

What should I wear?

For the morning ceremony, traditional Myanmar or formal attire. For the evening, black tie optional — midnight blues, champagne golds and a little sparkle are very welcome.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes. Complimentary parking is available at Lotus Garden Hall, and valet service runs from 5:30 PM for the reception.

Can I bring my children?

Little ones are warmly welcome at the ceremony and lunch. The evening reception is adults only — please let us know in your RSVP if that is a problem.

Do you have a gift registry?

Your presence is the greatest gift. If you would like to honour us further, a contribution towards our first home would be deeply appreciated.
