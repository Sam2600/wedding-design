Feature ideas

  • Personal guest links Built in

    Add ?to=Ko%20Zaw to the link and the page greets that guest by name. You can make one link per guest from a spreadsheet.

  • Add to calendar Built in

    One tap downloads an .ics file with both the ceremony and the reception, set to Yangon time.

  • RSVP with headcount Built in

    Accept or decline, number of guests and dietary notes, with validation. It isn't connected to anything yet. Formspree or Google Sheets would work.

  • Countdown Built in

    Numbers keep a fixed width so nothing jumps, and the timer pauses when the tab is hidden. After the wedding it changes to a "just married" message.

  • Wishes wall Idea

    Guests leave short blessings that show up as cards, either in a live feed or in a book you print after the wedding. Needs a small backend.

  • Gift QR Idea

    An optional section with a KBZPay or WavePay QR code for gifts from guests who can't come. You can hide it with a single setting.

  • Music toggle Idea

    Background music that starts only when the guest taps play, never automatically, and can be muted with an obvious button.

  • Live photo drop Idea

    After the day, guests upload their photos from a QR code printed on the table cards, and the gallery fills up with them.

  • Myanmar calendar date Idea

    Show the traditional Myanmar lunar date next to the regular date, as printed cards usually do. Have family check it before it goes out.
